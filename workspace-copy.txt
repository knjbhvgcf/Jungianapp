SPROUT TITLES AND ONE-LINES
---------------------------
INTJ  Mastermind
  Holds a long-range inner picture of how things should unfold, and then builds, in the shared world, the systems by which that picture might be made real without being sold too soon to mere usefulness.

INTP  Sage
  Takes ideas apart until the inner logic holds, and then branches, by extraverted intuition, into the new cases the model still cannot answer, preferring a true unfinished thought to a useful false close.

ENTJ  Architect-King
  Moves people and plans toward a visible goal, guided by a sense of the most effective path, and by an inner picture of where the whole campaign is tending, which extraverted thinking then undertakes to make happen.

ENTP  Trickster Inventor
  Opens possibilities in the object-world and in conversation, and then pressure-tests them for inner consistency, keeping the field from hardening too soon, and sometimes from hardening at all.

INFJ  Mystic
  Reads the deeper pattern in people and situations, and then works, by extraverted feeling, to bring the group toward a meaning that can almost be seen, as if the we had a soul that had not yet learned to speak.

INFP  Dreamer
  Protects an inner sense of what matters, often before it can be justified in public terms, and then explores the many lives in which that compass might still be true.

ENFJ  Mentor
  Gathers people around a shared feeling of purpose and an inner picture of who they could become together, hospitality here being not manners but a theory of the human, enacted.

ENFP  Wanderer
  Sees potential in people and ideas, and then checks, by a private standard of worth, which of those paths is still honest to pursue, spark remaining the method and sincerity the law.

ISTJ  Keeper of the Scrolls
  Keeps what has already been paid for in experience, and then organizes the present so that duties are met with care, duty itself being, in this stack, a form of love.

ISFJ  Heart Guardian
  Remembers what people need and what has been promised, and then maintains a climate of practical care, the house remaining a house because someone has stored, in the body, how it is kept.

ESTJ  Steward
  Sets standards, assigns roles, and uses proven methods so that the group actually delivers, trusting what can be shown, repeated, and handed on without a mystic preface.

ESFJ  Caretaker
  Tends relationships and shared customs so that everyone knows they belong and the day can run, hospitality being a craft by which the we is kept from coming apart.

ISTP  Craftsman
  Understands a system by taking it apart, and then acts on what the present situation actually requires, words remaining extra until they are a tool, and the object remaining the test.

ISFP  Artisan
  Moves from an inner sense of taste and worth, expressed through present, sensory, often quiet action, so that style can be a moral fact and the body a court of first instance.

ESTP  Warrior
  Reads the room and the moment, and then improvises a clever next move, life being a set of actual conditions rather than a briefing, timing the gift and later metabolizing the cost.

ESFP  Bard
  Brings energy, style, and feeling into the here and now, and then organizes enough to keep the moment alive, asking, if honest, whether the night was true as well as vivid.

    The supporting function. How you foster, teach, and take responsibility.
  Eternal Child:
    Play, relief, and inflation. Younger, less reliable, often charming.
  Inferior:
    The inferior function. Aspiration, embarrassment, and what you project onto others.
  Opposing Nemesis:
    The opposite attitude of the Hero / Heroine. Contrariness, “yes, but,” and defensive spin.
  Critic:
    The shadow of the Parent. Criticism, limit-setting, and bitterness.
  Trickster:
    The shadow of the Eternal Child. Double binds, mischief, and escape hatches.
  Demon:
    The most unconscious. Undermining — and, in later work, a source of transformation.
