Ni — Introverted Intuition  (8 questions)
========================================================================

Ni-1
Inner images show me the direction of a situation before the outer facts are complete.

Ni-2
Events interest me mainly as signs of a pattern I cannot yet point to in the room.

Ni-3
I often arrive at an inner conviction and only later reconstruct the steps that led there.

Ni-4
I would rather follow one unfolding inner vision than keep many outer options in play.

Ni-5
I sense where something is tending as if I had already seen the end of the story.

Ni-6 reverse
I rarely know where a situation is going until the outer facts have already lined up.

Ni-7
I tend to keep my hopes and dreams close to me, and don't enjoy sharing prospective ideas with people close to me.

Ni-8
I like to explore ideas alone but when it comes to doing the work I like to do it in a group.

Ne — Extraverted Intuition  (8 questions)
========================================================================

Ne-1
I notice what a situation could become, not only what it presently is.

Ne-2
One outer fact quickly suggests several other paths that are not yet real.

Ne-3
I scent connections between things that have not been linked yet.

Ne-4
Once a possibility has been realized, my interest often moves on to the next one.

Ne-5
I feel most alive when the field of outer options is still open.

Ne-6 reverse
Once I have one path that could work, I do not go looking for other ones.

Ne-7
I enjoy telling people close to me about all the different possibilities and opportunities that excite me.

Ne-8
I like to explore ideas with people but when it comes to doing the work I like to be alone and go inwards.

Si — Introverted Sensing  (6 questions)
========================================================================

Si-1
What I notice first is the impression an object leaves in me, not only its public properties.

Si-2
The present is vivid to me when it echoes something I have already lived in the body.

Si-3
I judge a new situation by how it compares with my inner store of sensory memory.

Si-4
Familiar sequences and bodily baselines tell me whether something is “as it should be.”

Si-5
A thing can look ordinary to others and still carry a dense private texture for me.

Si-6 reverse
I do not check a new moment against how this kind of moment has felt before.

========================================================================
Se — Extraverted Sensing  (6 questions)
========================================================================

Se-1
I trust the concrete facts of this moment more than a theory about them.

Se-2
Texture, movement, timing, and impact tell me what is real.

Se-3
I would rather act on what is here than wait for a hidden meaning.

Se-4
My attention locks onto the actual object or scene in front of me.

Se-5
Pleasure, pain, and aesthetic impact in the present guide my next move.

Se-6 reverse
I often take in the room only afterwards, if I notice the scene at all.

========================================================================
Ti — Introverted Thinking  (6 questions)
========================================================================

Ti-1
I need the idea itself to be consistent, even if it is not yet useful in the world.

Ti-2
I measure claims against an inner model of how the thing must work.

Ti-3
A popular explanation bothers me if it is logically hollow.

Ti-4
I would rather refine a definition than apply a ready-made formula.

Ti-5
I think in order to understand, not first of all in order to arrange the outer world.

Ti-6 reverse
I can accept an explanation that works well enough even if I cannot make it consistent.

========================================================================
Te — Extraverted Thinking  (6 questions)
========================================================================

Te-1
I trust a conclusion more when it follows from data others can also check.

Te-2
I organize people, time, and facts according to an objective standard.

Te-3
An argument that cannot be put to a practical test soon loses me.

Te-4
I want thinking to produce an order that works in the shared world.

Te-5
Results that can be shown matter more to me than the elegance of an inner theory.

Te-6 reverse
I can leave a plan unarranged if the inner idea still feels unfinished.

========================================================================
Fi — Introverted Feeling  (6 questions)
========================================================================

Fi-1
A choice is wrong for me if it violates a private value, even when it is popular.

Fi-2
My feeling about something can be intense while little of it shows.

Fi-3
I know what I love or reject before I can justify it in public terms.

Fi-4
I would rather stay loyal to an inner standard than to good manners.

Fi-5
Collective taste does not settle whether something is right for me.

Fi-6 reverse
I can set a private value aside when keeping it would make things awkward.

========================================================================
Fe — Extraverted Feeling  (6 questions)
========================================================================

Fe-1
I know what is fitting by reading the feeling-tone of the people involved.

Fe-2
I adjust what I show so that the relation between us stays intact.

Fe-3
What “one” does — the received value of the group — weighs on my choices.

Fe-4
I feel responsible for whether the atmosphere between people is bearable.

Fe-5
A decision that is logically neat still feels unfinished if it injures the group.

Fe-6 reverse
I do not take the room’s mood as a reason to change what I will do.

Working pairs  (how two functions work together)
========================================================================

--- Ni-Te ---

Ni-Te-1
I see where things are heading, then I build a plan or system that can actually carry that picture.

Ni-Te-2
An inner vision is not finished until it has a working order in the world — steps, calendars, results other people can check.

--- Ni-Fe ---

Ni-Fe-1
I see where things are heading, then I try to bring people with me toward a meaning we can share.

Ni-Fe-2
An inner vision is not finished until it has a human climate it can live in — a we, a tone, a way of being together.

--- Ne-Ti ---

Ne-Ti-1
I open many possibilities, then I pressure-test them until the idea itself holds together.

Ne-Ti-2
A new option only counts if I can take it apart and see whether the logic is sound.

--- Ne-Fi ---

Ne-Fi-1
I open many possibilities, then I check which ones still feel honest to me.

Ne-Fi-2
A new option only counts if it still sits right with my own sense of what matters.

--- Si-Te ---

Si-Te-1
I rely on what has already proved itself, then I put that known good into a working order others can follow.

Si-Te-2
Experience becomes useful when it is turned into a standard, a procedure, a way things get done.

--- Si-Fe ---

Si-Fe-1
I rely on what has already proved itself, then I use it to keep people comfortable and cared for.

Si-Fe-2
Experience becomes useful when it tells me what this person, this house, this group needs to remain well.

--- Se-Ti ---

Se-Ti-1
I read the actual scene in front of me, then I find the clever angle that will work on this object, now.

Se-Ti-2
What is real is what I can do with my hands and my timing — the model has to fit the thing in front of me.

--- Se-Fi ---

Se-Fi-1
I read the actual scene in front of me, then I ask whether this moment is still true to what I care about.

Se-Fi-2
What is real is presence — the look, the night, the body — and I will not fake a feeling that is not mine.

--- Ti-Ne ---

Ti-Ne-1
I build an inner model of how something works, then I hunt for new cases that could break or complete it.

Ti-Ne-2
Understanding comes first; I keep the laboratory open and feed it more possibilities rather than closing on a useful answer too soon.

--- Ti-Se ---

Ti-Se-1
I build an inner model of how something works, then I test it on the actual object with my hands.

Ti-Se-2
Understanding comes first; I take the thing apart and use it until the model and the object agree.

--- Te-Ni ---

Te-Ni-1
I organize people and facts toward a result, guided by a long inner picture of where this should go.

Te-Ni-2
The plan is in service of a destination I already see — not a to-do list that wandered in without an end.

--- Te-Si ---

Te-Si-1
I organize people and facts toward a result, using methods that have already worked.

Te-Si-2
The plan is in service of a proven standard — what can be repeated, handed on, and shown.

--- Fi-Ne ---

Fi-Ne-1
I protect a private sense of what is right, then I look for lives and paths in which that compass could still be true.

Fi-Ne-2
The inner yes-or-no comes first; the outer world is a field of maybe’s I search for a place that still feels like me.

--- Fi-Se ---

Fi-Se-1
I protect a private sense of what is right, then I put it into this moment — a gesture, a craft, a way of being in the room.

Fi-Se-2
The inner yes-or-no comes first; style and presence are how that value becomes real without a speech.

--- Fe-Ni ---

Fe-Ni-1
I read the feeling of the group, then I hold a picture of who we could become together.

Fe-Ni-2
The we comes first; I work toward a shared future that has a face, not only a pleasant evening.

--- Fe-Si ---

Fe-Si-1
I read the feeling of the group, then I keep the customs and care that make belonging possible.

Fe-Si-2
The we comes first; I remember who needs what, and what the house needs to remain a house.
